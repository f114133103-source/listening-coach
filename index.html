How to play

  1. Tap Play audio and listen to the conversation.
  2. Answer the mission questions. On phones, tap an answer or choose an activity from the list.
  3. Use Get a hint or replay the audio if you get stuck.
  4. Complete a mission to earn stars and unlock the next level.

Audio is generated with your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. No audio download or account is needed. Use the volume buttons on your device.
